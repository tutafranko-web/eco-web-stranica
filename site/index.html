Velo Varoš

The oldest part of Split

Stone lanes west of Diocletian's Palace, a small square by the Church of the Holy Cross — and a door that opens into a warm room.

Velo Varoš is the old fishermen's and stonemasons' quarter: narrow streets, stone houses, stairs climbing towards Marjan. PIER is a small bistro in it that runs all day — from the morning coffee and breakfast, through lunch, to a dinner that stretches to midnight.
